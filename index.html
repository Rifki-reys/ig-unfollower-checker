About

A privacy-first checker, not a tracker

There is no official Instagram endpoint that hands you your own follower list in a readable form, so people keep giving their login to sketchy sites. This page is the opposite of that: plain HTML, plain JavaScript, no accounts, no server, no analytics on your file.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The ZIP is opened with JSZip inside your browser and the JSON is matched locally. Refreshing or closing the tab discards everything — there is no storage and no backend.

Do I need to log in or hand over my password?

Never. The tool has no sign-in, no OAuth and no password field. It only accepts files you already downloaded yourself from Meta.

Where do I find followers_1.json and following.json?

Inside the extracted ZIP they live in connections/followers_and_following/. You can also pick the two files directly with the JSON buttons — no need to zip anything.

What does a second export add?

Upload an older export as well and the tool diffs them: who started following you, who stopped following you, and who you unfollowed who never followed back. Add the newest export last.

Why is the number different from the app?

Instagram counts deactivated, private and blocked accounts in some places and not others, and your export can lag by hours or days. A small difference is normal.
